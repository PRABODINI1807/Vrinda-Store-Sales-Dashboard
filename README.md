# Vrinda Store Annual Sales Dashboard
An interactive one-page Excel dashboard built from raw Vrinda Store sales data(2022), with 6 KPI cards and 6 charts.

## Problem
The store's raw sales data was spread across many rows and columns, which made it hard to spot trends or decide where to focus.This dashboard turns it into one page of clear, filterable insights.

## Tools Used
Excel, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, KPI cards, Data Cleaning

## What I Did
- Cleaned the raw sales data (fixed inconsistencies and standardised columns)
- Built Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts to answer each business question
- Added Slicers (Category, Month, Channel) to filter the KPI and charts dynamically
- Created 6 KPI cards: Total Sales, Total Orders, Net revenue, Avg Order value, Delivery Rate and Cancellation Rate
- Designed a one-page dashboard with 6 KPI cards and 6 charts

## Questions Answered
1. Compare sales and orders using a single chart
2. Which month had the highest sales and orders?
3. Who purchased more, men or women?
4. What are the different order statuses?
5. Which are the top 10 states contributing to sales?
6. What is the relationship between age and gender based on number of orders?
7. Which channel contributes the most sales & orders?
8. What is the overall business performane(sales,revenue,order value and delivery/cancellation rates)?

## Key Insights
- The store made 21.18M in total sales from 31,047 orders, with an average order value of about 688 and a net revenue of 19.71M
- 92.25% of orders were delivered and only 2.72% were cancelled;the rest were returned or refunded
- Women contribute 64% of sales, and adult women are the largest buyer group (34.59% of orders)
- Sales and orders peaked in **March**, then declined steadily to their lowest levels in Nov-Dec
- **Maharashtra, Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh** are the top 3 states by sales
- **Amazon (35%), Myntra (23%) and Flipkart (22%)** bring in most orders

## Recommendations
- Focus marketing and new collections on women buyers, especially adults
- Invest more in the top 3 states and test campaigns in lower-performing ones
- Prioritize Amazon, Myntra and Flipkart for promotions and stock planning
- Investigate the sales drop in Nov-Dec and run seasonal offers to recover it
- Find the main reasons for returns and cancellations and reduce them to protect net revenue
